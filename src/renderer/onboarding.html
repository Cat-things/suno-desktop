A few things worth knowing:

  • Your media keys just work. Play, pause and skip from the keyboard, the Windows media overlay or the taskbar thumbnail.
  • Closing keeps the music going. Suno Desktop waits in the tray; quit from the tray icon when you're done.
  • Settings are one shortcut away (Ctrl + ,). Discord Rich Presence lives there too, off until you turn it on.
